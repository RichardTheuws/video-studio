# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone contributing to Video Studio — regardless of experience level, background, or identity. Be kind, be constructive, assume good faith.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Issues can be reported to **richard@theuws.com**. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may block repeat offenders.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
